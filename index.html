MUSIC WITHOUT BORDERS

Pop has no single hometown. Its songs travel from Seoul to São Paulo, Kingston to London, crossing languages and drawing from R&B, dance, rock, hip-hop and sounds that refuse to fit one genre.

Thaalam 24x7 celebrates that exchange: the artists who shape a global sound, and the listeners who make a song feel at home anywhere. From stadium anthems to late-night discoveries, every voice adds something new to the mix.

Independent, fan-made and open source, this project is built around one simple idea: the right song can make the world feel a little closer. Find a familiar voice, discover a new one, and stay with the music.
